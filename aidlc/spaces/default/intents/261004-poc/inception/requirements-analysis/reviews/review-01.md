### Summary

Requirements trace well to the intent and to the Q1 to Q8 answers, and most items have concrete pass lines. Two Major gaps could block building the tab check and the time-driven lifecycle without guessing: partner-side visibility and tab placement after a withdrawal, and the time edge cases. Both have clear workarounds, and the other items are small clarifications, so this is READY with those gaps for the human to weigh. Better to resolve R-01 and R-02 before Code Generation, since no design stage follows.
