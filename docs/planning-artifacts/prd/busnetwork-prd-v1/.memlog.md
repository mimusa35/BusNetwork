- (decision) The PRD adds the detail engineering, UX and QA need and nothing that changes the BRD. Precedence: BRD › PRD › SPEC/Architecture › UX. Detail is labelled: DR-nn (derived requirement the BRD makes necessary), WA-nn (working assumption where the BRD is silent, each with a PQ), AC-… (acceptance criteria).
- (decision) Two records, not one: the BRD's six statuses describe the trip; the product also keeps one share request per partner, and a partner's company-view status is derived from its own request (§6.2). This keeps BR-01 and BN-ALL-03 true for every company. Tracked as PQ-01.
- (decision) Gaps in the BRD are carried as working assumptions with a question rather than silently decided. Open for decision: PQ-01 partner status mapping, PQ-02 tab visibility, PQ-04 who may invoice, PQ-05 who may mark complete, PQ-10 deadline default, PQ-15 customer data a partner may see, PQ-13 currency, plus PQ-03, 06, 07, 08, 09, 11, 14, 16..21. PQ-12 was merged into PQ-11 during drafting; its ID is not reused.
- (decision) Coverage rule: every BRD requirement has a PRD section, a traceability row (§18.1) and at least one acceptance criterion (§13) — verified by script at 66/66.
